Facebook Quick Start Guide

Version 1.0 | Audience: New Users

1. What Is Facebook?

Facebook is the one of the worlds largest social networking platform, with over three billion monthly active users. It lets you connect with friends and family, discover and share content, join interest-based communities, follow public figures and businesses, and stay informed about events in your world.

This quickstart guide will help you to creating Your Facebook Account, setting up your profile, and connecting with people.

2. Prerequisites

Ensure you have the following items:

Requirement Detail

3. Creating Your Facebook Account

3.1 Sign Up on the Web

  1. Open your browser and go to https://www.facebook.com.
  2. Click Create new account.
  3. Enter your first name and last name.
  4. Enter your mobile number or email address.
  5. Create a strong password. The password must be at least 8 characters, mixing letters, numbers, and symbols.
  6. Enter your date of birth using the drop-down menus.
  7. Select your gender or choose custom to specify your own.
  8. Click Sign Up.
  9. Check your email or phone for a confirmation code from Facebook and enter it when prompted.

3.2 Confirming Your Identity

Facebook may ask you to verify your identity through one or more of the following methods:

  • Email confirmation – Click the link in the verification email sent to your address.
  • SMS code – Enter the 6-digit code sent to your mobile phone.
  • Photo ID – In rare cases, Facebook may request a government-issued ID to confirm your identity.

Privacy Note: Facebook encrypts your password and never displays it to other users. Use a unique password. Do not reuse it on other websites.

4. Setting Up Your Profile

Set up your profile to personalize your Facebook experience, help friends find you, and build trust within your network.

4.1 Add a Profile Photo

Add a profile photo to help friends easily recognize your account.

Note: Use a clear, front-facing photo so friends can easily recognize you. Profile photos are always public.

To add a profile photo,

  1. At the top of any page, click your profile icon.
  2. Click Add Profile Picture.
  3. To upload an image from your device, choose Upload Photo. To use your camera, choose Take Photo.
  4. Drag the image to reposition it within the cropping area, and then click Save.

You have successfully added your profile photo.

4.2 Add a Cover Photo

Add a cover photo that displays your individual, brand, or organization.

To add a cover photo,

  1. Navigate to your profile page, on the large banner area click the camera icon.
  2. Select Upload Photo or choose from your existing Facebook photos.
  3. Reposition the image and click Save Changes.

4.3 Complete Your Bio

To complete your profile, on your profile page click Edit profile to enter the following information:

Section What to include
Work Current employer, job title, and previous positions
Education High school, college, or university attended
Current city Where you live now
Hometown Where you grew up
Relationship status Single, in a relationship, married, etc. (optional)
Bio A short description of yourself (up to 101 characters)
Pronouns How you identify (optional, visible to friends or everyone)
Websites and social links Personal website, LinkedIn, Instagram, etc.

Note: Each profile field has its own privacy control. You choose who sees each piece of information: Everyone, Friends, Friends of Friends, or Only Me.

5. Managing Privacy and Security Settings

Managing privacy settings helps you to control who can see your content.

5.1 Access Privacy Settings

To access your privacy settings,

  1. On the top right corner, click the down-arrow icon.
  2. Go to Settings & Privacyand select Settings.
  3. On the left pane, click Privacy.

5.3 Enable Two-Factor Authentication (2FA)

Two-factor authentication (2FA) reduces the risk of unauthorized access to accounts by requiring two distinct forms of identity verification—something you know (like a password), something you have (like a smartphone or security key), or something you are (like a fingerprint). This added layer significantly diminishes the impact of common cyber threats such as phishing, credential stuffing, brute-force attacks, and password theft, even if login credentials are compromised.

To enable two-factor authentication (2FA),

  1. Go to Settings > Security and Login.
  2. Under Two-Factor Authentication, click Edit.
  3. Select your preferred method: Authentication App (recommended) or SMS.
  4. Follow the prompts to complete setup.

7. Managing Friend Requests

7.1 To accept a request

Note: Add only individuals whom you know. Accepting unknown friend requests can expose your posts and personal information to strangers.

To accept a friend request,

  1. On the top navigation, Go to the Friends icon.
  2. Click Confirm to accept the request.

The person will get a notification that you accepted their request and are now friends.

7.2 To decline a request

To decline a friend request,

  1. On the top navigation, Go to the Friends icon.
  2. Click Delete Request.

The request will be removed and the person will not get a notification.

7.3 To remove a friend

To remove a friend,

  1. Go to the friends profile.
  2. On the top navigation, click Friends and select Unfriend.

The person will no longer appear in your friend list. The person will not get a notification.

8. Understanding Your News Feed

Your News Feed is the primary page displayed after logging in.

On the News Feed, you can view and interact with posts from friends, Pages you follow, Groups you have joined, and content from sponsors.

The Facebook algorithm ranks content based on your interactions, so posts from people you engage with most will appear higher in your feed.

9. Creating a Post

A post is a status update, a photo, a video, or a link to another website.

To create a post,

  1. On the top of your News Feed or profile, click the What is on your mind? box.
  2. Enter your text. You can also add one or more of the following:
    • Photo/Video : Upload media from your device
    • Feeling/Activity : Share your mood or what you are doing
    • Tag People : Mention friends so they are linked in the post
    • Check In : Share your current location
    • Background Color : Add a colored background to text-only posts
  3. Select your audience: Friends, Public, Only Me, or a custom list.
  4. Click Post.

The post will be published and visible to your selected audience.

12. Managing Facebook Groups

Facebook Groups are community spaces built around shared interests, causes, or affiliations.

12.1 Find Facebook Groups

To find Facebook groups, use one of the below options:

  • Groups Menu : On the left pane, click Groups.
  • Search Bar : At the top of the page, use the search bar to search for groups by name or topic.
  • Discover : In the Groups section, click Discover to browse categories like Suggested for You or Popular Near You.

12.2 Join Facebook Groups

To join Facebook groups,

  1. On the left pane, click Groups.
  2. (optional) On the top of the page, use the search bar to search for groups by name or topic.
  3. Click on the group you want to join. If it's a public group, to its page, you can see posts and the group description.
  4. Click Join.

    Notes: If the group is public, you will be added immediately. If it is private or closed, your request will be sent to the admins for approval.

    Notes: If you are joining as a Facebook Page, you will be asked to select whether to join as your personal profile or your Page. Not all groups allow Pages to join. This depends on the groups settings.

The group will be added to your list of joined groups. You will see posts from the group in your feed.

12.3 Create a Group

To create a group,

  1. On the left pane, click Groups.
  2. At the top of the page, click Create Group.
  3. Enter the group name and select Next.
  4. Select a group type and click Next.
  5. Enter a group description and click Next.
  6. Choose who can post and click Next.
  7. Review your group information and click Create.

The group will be created and you will be taken to the group dashboard.

13. Managing Facebook Pages

Facebook Pages are public profiles for businesses, brands, celebrities, and organizations.

13.1 Find Facebook Pages

To find Facebook pages, use one of the below options:

  • Search Bar : At the top of the page, use the search bar to search for pages by name or topic.
  • Discover : In the Pages section, click Discover to browse categories like Suggested for You or Popular Near You.
  • Browse : In the Pages section, click Browse to view pages by category or directory.
  • Saved : In the Pages section, click Saved to view pages you have saved for later.
  • View All : In the Pages section, click View All to view all the pages you follow.

13.2 Follow Facebook Pages

To follow Facebook pages,

  1. On the left pane, click Pages.
  2. (optional) On the top of the page, use the search bar to search for pages by name or topic.
  3. Click on the page you want to follow. The page will open.
  4. Click Follow.

The page will be added to your list of followed pages. You will see updates from the page in your feed.

13.3 Create a Facebook Page

To create a Facebook page,

  1. On the left pane, click Pages.
  2. At the top of the page, click Create Page.
  3. Enter the page name and select Next.
  4. Select a page category and click Next.
  5. Enter a page description and click Next.
  6. Review your page information and click Create.

The page will be created and you will be taken to the page dashboard.

14. Managing Facebook Events

Facebook Events are a way to announce and celebrate events, from birthday parties to charity fundraisers.

13.1 Find Facebook Events

To find Facebook events, use one of the below options:

  • Search Bar : At the top of the page, use the search bar to search for events by name or topic.
  • Discover : In the Events section, click Discover to browse categories like Suggested for You or Popular Near You.
  • Browse : In the Events section, click Browse to view events by category or directory.
  • Saved : In the Events section, click Saved to view events you have saved for later.
  • View All : In the Events section, click View All to view all the events you are interested in.

13.2 Attend Facebook Events

To attend Facebook events,

  1. On the left pane, click Events.
  2. (optional) On the top of the page, use the search bar to search for events by name or topic.
  3. Click on the event you want to attend. The event page will open.
  4. Click Going.

You will be added to the list of attendees.

13.3 Create a Facebook Event

To create a Facebook event,

  1. On the left pane, click Events.
  2. At the top of the page, click Create Event.
  3. Enter the event name and select Next.
  4. Select the event type and click Next.
  5. Enter the event description and click Next.
  6. Review your event information and click Create.

The event will be created and you will be taken to the event dashboard.

14. Troubleshooting Common Issues

The following table lists common issues and their solutions.

Problem Solution
Forgot your password On the login page, select Forgot Password. Enter your email or phone, and select the reset link to continue.
Locked account The system locks accounts for policy violations or shows suspicious activity. Go to facebook.com/login/identify to begin the account recovery process.
Unable to find a friend Ask your friend to check their privacy settings to ensure others can search for them.
Missing post from feed A post may be missing if it was deleted or if the audience settings excluded you. Ask the poster directly for help.
Irrelevant News Feed Use the three-dot menu on posts to select Hide Post or Snooze [Name] for 30 days to improve feed relevance.
Excessive notifications Go to Settings and select Notifications to customize or reduce the notification types you receive.
Unauthorized login Go to Settings and select Security and Login then choose Where You’re Logged In. Log out of any unrecognized sessions and change your password immediately.

15. Staying Safe on Facebook

Important: Facebook will never ask for your password in email or Messenger. Do not trust messages that ask for your sign in information.

Follow these best practices to keep your account and personal information safe:

  • Use a unique strong password and change it ever 6 to 12 months.
  • Enable two-factor authentication. Refer to Section 5.3.
  • Review your active sessions regularly. Refer to Section 5.4.
  • Do not click links in messages that the user did not request.
  • Report suspicious accounts, spam, or inappropriate content using the three-dot menu on any post or profile.
  • Be cautious about sharing your phone number, home address, or daily routine publicly.
  • Use the Privacy Checkup tool to audit your settings every few months. Refer to Section 5.5.
