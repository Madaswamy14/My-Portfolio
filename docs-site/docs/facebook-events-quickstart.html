Facebook Quick Start Guide

Set up your profile, connect with people, and get the most out of Facebook in minutes.

Version 1.0 | Audience: New Users

1. What Is Facebook?

Facebook is the world’s largest social networking platform, with over three billion monthly active users. It lets you connect with friends and family, discover and share content, join interest-based communities, follow public figures and businesses, and stay informed about events in your world.

This Quick Start Guide covers everything you need to be up and running — from account creation to your first post.

2. Before You Begin

Confirm you have the following before creating your account:

Requirement Detail
Device Smartphone (iOS 16+ or Android 8+), tablet, or desktop computer
Browser (desktop) Chrome 90+, Firefox 90+, Edge 90+, or Safari 14+
Mobile App Facebook app from the App Store or Google Play (recommended for mobile)
Email or Phone A valid email address or mobile phone number for registration
Age You must be at least 13 years old to create a Facebook account
Internet Connection Stable Wi-Fi or mobile data connection

3. Creating Your Facebook Account

3.1 Sign Up on the Web

  1. Open your browser and go to https://www.facebook.com.
  2. Click Create new account.
  3. Enter your First name and Last name.
  4. Enter your mobile number or email address.
  5. Create a strong password (at least 8 characters, mixing letters, numbers, and symbols).
  6. Enter your Date of birth using the dropdown menus.
  7. Select your Gender (or choose Custom to specify your own).
  8. Click Sign Up.
  9. Check your email or phone for a confirmation code from Facebook and enter it when prompted.

📱 Mobile App: Download the Facebook app and tap Create new account. The steps are identical to the web signup flow above.

3.2 Confirming Your Identity

Facebook may ask you to verify your identity through one or more of the following methods:

  • Email confirmation – Click the link in the verification email sent to your address.
  • SMS code – Enter the 6-digit code sent to your mobile phone.
  • Photo ID – In rare cases, Facebook may request a government-issued ID to confirm your identity.

🔒 Privacy Note: Facebook encrypts your password and never displays it to other users. Use a unique password you do not use on other websites.

4. Setting Up Your Profile

Your profile is your personal space on Facebook. A complete profile helps friends find you and builds trust with your network.

4.1 Add a Profile Photo

  1. Tap or click your profile icon (top of any page).
  2. Click Add Profile Picture.
  3. Choose Upload Photo to use an image from your device, or Take Photo to use your camera.
  4. Drag to reposition the image in the crop circle, then click Save.

💡 Tip: Use a clear, front-facing photo so friends can easily recognize you. Profile photos are always public.

4.2 Add a Cover Photo

  1. Navigate to your profile page and click the camera icon on the large banner area.
  2. Select Upload Photo or choose from your existing Facebook photos.
  3. Reposition the image and click Save Changes.

4.3 Complete Your Bio

Click Edit profile on your profile page to fill in the following sections:

Section What to Include
Work Current employer, job title, and previous positions
Education High school, college, or university attended
Current City Where you live now
Hometown Where you grew up
Relationship Status Single, in a relationship, married, etc. (optional)
Bio A short description of yourself (up to 101 characters)
Pronouns How you identify (optional, visible to friends or everyone)
Websites & Social Links Personal website, LinkedIn, Instagram, etc.

🔍 Note: Each profile field has its own privacy control. You choose who sees each piece of information: Everyone, Friends, Friends of Friends, or Only Me.

5. Privacy and Security Settings

Before adding friends or posting, review your privacy settings to control who can see your content.

5.1 Access Privacy Settings

  1. Click the menu icon (three horizontal lines on mobile) or the down-arrow icon on desktop.
  2. Go to Settings & Privacy > Settings.
  3. Click Privacy in the left sidebar.

5.2 Key Privacy Controls

Setting Location Recommended Value
Who can see your future posts? Privacy > Your Activity Friends
Who can send you friend requests? Privacy > How People Find You Friends of Friends
Who can look you up by email? Privacy > How People Find You Friends
Who can look you up by phone? Privacy > How People Find You Friends
Do you want search engines to link to your profile? Privacy > How People Find You Off (No)
Profile picture visibility Your Profile > Edit Friends (not Public)

5.3 Enable Two-Factor Authentication (2FA)

  1. Go to Settings > Security and Login.
  2. Under Two-Factor Authentication, click Edit.
  3. Select your preferred method: Authentication App (recommended) or SMS.
  4. Follow the prompts to complete setup.

🔐 Security Best Practice: Enable two-factor authentication immediately after creating your account. It significantly reduces the risk of unauthorized access.

6. Finding and Adding Friends

6.1 Ways to Find Friends

Method How to Use It
Search Bar Type a person’s name in the top search bar. Click their profile, then Add Friend.
People You May Know Facebook suggests connections based on mutual friends and shared info.
Import Contacts Go to Friends > Find Friends to sync your phone contacts or email address book.
Facebook Groups Search for groups related to your workplace, school, or interests and connect with members.
Facebook Events View attendees of events you’re interested in and send friend requests.

6.2 Managing Friend Requests

  • To accept a request: Go to the Friends icon in the top nav and click Confirm.
  • To decline a request: Click Delete Request. The person is not notified.
  • To remove a friend: Visit their profile, click Friends, then select Unfriend. They are not notified.

💡 Tip: Only add people you actually know. Accepting unknown friend requests can expose your posts and personal information to strangers.

7. The News Feed and Posting

7.1 Understanding Your News Feed

Your News Feed is the main page you see after logging in. It shows a personalized stream of posts from friends, Pages you follow, Groups you’ve joined, and sponsored content.

Facebook’s algorithm ranks content based on your interactions, so posts from people you engage with most will appear higher in your feed.

7.2 Creating a Post

  1. Click the What’s on your mind? box at the top of your News Feed or profile.
  2. Type your text. You can also add one or more of the following:
    • Photo/Video – Upload media from your device
    • Feeling/Activity – Share your mood or what you’re doing
    • Tag People – Mention friends so they’re linked in the post
    • Check In – Share your current location
    • Background Color – Add a colored background to text-only posts
  3. Set your post audience using the audience selector (Friends, Public, Only Me, or a custom list).
  4. Click Post to publish.

7.3 Post Privacy Options

Audience Setting Who Can See the Post
Public Anyone on or off Facebook, including people not logged in
Friends Only your confirmed Facebook friends
Friends except... All friends minus specific people you exclude
Specific friends Only the people you manually select
Only me Visible only to you — useful for drafts or private notes
Custom Combine any of the above with include/exclude logic

8. Key Features to Explore

8.1 Facebook Groups

Groups are community spaces built around shared interests, causes, or affiliations.

  • To find groups: Click Groups in the left sidebar > Discover, or search for a topic in the search bar.
  • To join a group: Click Join Group. Public groups are instant; private groups require admin approval.
  • To create a group: Click + Create > Group, give it a name, set privacy (Public or Private), and invite members.

8.2 Facebook Pages

Pages are public profiles for businesses, brands, celebrities, and organizations.

  • To follow a Page: Visit any Page and click Follow. Their posts will appear in your feed.
  • To create a Page: Click Pages > Create New Page. Choose a category, name, and description.

8.3 Facebook Marketplace

Marketplace lets you buy and sell items locally or through Facebook’s delivery partners.

  1. Click Marketplace in the left sidebar.
  2. Browse listings by category, or search by keyword.
  3. Click a listing to view details and message the seller.
  4. To sell: Click + Create New Listing, choose a category, add photos, set a price, and publish.

8.4 Facebook Events

  • To find events: Click Events in the left sidebar to see local, online, and friend-organized events.
  • To respond: Click Interested or Going on any event page.
  • To create an event: Click + Create Event, fill in the name, date, time, location, and description, then invite guests.

8.5 Facebook Messenger

Messenger is Facebook’s built-in messaging service for private conversations.

  • Access Messenger by clicking the chat icon at the top of the page, or download the Messenger app separately.
  • Start a conversation: Click New Message, search for a friend, and type your message.
  • You can send text, photos, videos, voice messages, stickers, reactions, and money (in supported regions).

9. Using the Facebook Mobile App

The Facebook mobile app (available for iOS and Android) provides a faster, notification-enabled experience optimized for phones.

9.1 App Navigation (Bottom Tab Bar)

Tab Icon Function
Home (house) Your News Feed — the main content stream
Video (play) Facebook Watch — short and long-form videos
Marketplace (shop tag) Buy and sell locally
Notifications (bell) Likes, comments, friend requests, and alerts
Menu (three lines) Access all other features: Groups, Events, Memories, Settings

9.2 Managing App Notifications

  1. Tap the Menu icon > Settings & Privacy > Settings.
  2. Tap Notifications.
  3. Toggle individual notification types on or off (e.g., Friend Requests, Comments, Tags, Live Videos).

📱 Tip: Turn off push notifications for categories you don’t need to reduce distractions. At minimum, keep Friend Request and Security Alert notifications enabled.

10. Troubleshooting Common Issues

Problem Solution
Forgot your password Click Forgot Password on the login page, enter your email or phone, and follow the reset link.
Account is locked Facebook locks accounts that violate policies or show suspicious activity. Go to facebook.com/login/identify to begin the recovery process.
Cannot find a friend Ask them to check their privacy settings. They may have restricted who can search for them.
Post is missing from feed The post may have been deleted or the audience was set to exclude you. Ask the poster directly.
News Feed feels irrelevant Use the three-dot menu on posts to select Hide Post or Snooze [Name] for 30 days to train the algorithm.
Receiving too many notifications Go to Settings > Notifications to customize or reduce the types you receive.
Suspected unauthorized login Go to Settings > Security and Login > Where You’re Logged In and log out of any unrecognized sessions. Change your password immediately.

11. Staying Safe on Facebook

⚠️ Important: Facebook will never ask for your password via email or Messenger. Any message claiming to be from Facebook and requesting your credentials is a phishing attempt.

Follow these best practices to keep your account and personal information safe:

  • Use a unique, strong password and update it every 6–12 months.
  • Enable two-factor authentication (see Section 5.3).
  • Review your active sessions regularly (Settings > Security and Login).
  • Do not click links in unsolicited messages, even from people you know.
  • Report suspicious accounts, spam, or inappropriate content using the three-dot menu on any post or profile.
  • Be cautious about sharing your phone number, home address, or daily routine publicly.
  • Use the Privacy Checkup tool (Settings > Privacy > Privacy Checkup) to audit your settings every few months.

12. Next Steps

You’re ready to get the most out of Facebook. Here are some suggested next steps:

  • Complete your Privacy Checkup under Settings > Privacy > Privacy Checkup.
  • Join 2–3 Groups related to your hobbies, profession, or local community.
  • Follow Pages for news sources, brands, or public figures you find interesting.
  • Explore Facebook Watch for videos personalized to your interests.
  • Set up Facebook Pay if you want to send money to friends or shop on Marketplace.
  • Download the Facebook app (if you haven’t already) for on-the-go access and notifications.

📚 Help Center: For detailed help on any topic, visit https://www.facebook.com/help — Facebook’s official support portal with guides, troubleshooting wizards, and community forums.
