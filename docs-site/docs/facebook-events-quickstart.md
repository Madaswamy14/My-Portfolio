# Facebook Quickstart Guide

**Version:** 1.0  
**Audience:** New Users  

## 1. Introduction
Facebook is the one of the worlds largest social networking platform, with over three billion monthly active users. It lets you connect with friends and family, discover and share content, join interest-based communities, follow public figures and businesses, and stay informed about events in your world.
This quickstart guide will help you to create your Facebook Account, set up your profile, and connect with people.

## 2. Prerequisites
Ensure you have the following items:
| Requirement | Detail |
| --- | --- |
| Device | Smartphone with iOS 16+ or Android 8+, tablet, or desktop computer |
| Browser | Chrome 90+, Firefox 90+, Edge 90+, or Safari 14+ |
| Email or phone | A valid email address or mobile phone number for registration |
| Age | Users must be thirteen years of age or older to join Facebook |
| Internet connection | Stable Wi-Fi or mobile-data connection |

## 3. Create a Facebook Account
### 3.1 Sign Up on the Web
This procedure explains how to create a Facebook account on the web.
**Prerequisites:**
* A web browser such as Google Chrome, Mozilla Firefox, or Apple Safari.
* An email address or mobile phone number to use for account verification.
To create,
1. Open your browser and go to https://www.facebook.com.
2. On the home page, select **Create new account**.
3. Enter your first name and last name.
4. Enter your mobile number or email address.
5. Enter a strong password. The password must be at least 8 characters, mixing letters, numbers, and symbols.
6. Select your date of birth.
7. Select your gender.
8. Select **Sign Up**.
9. Check your email or phone for a confirmation code from Facebook.
10. On the verification screen, enter the confirmation code.
You have successfully created a Facebook account

### 3.2 Confirm Your Identity
This procedure explains how to verify your identity when you create a Facebook account.
**Prerequisites:**
* A Facebook account.
* Access to the email address or phone number used for account registration.
To verify,
* **Email confirmation** : In your email inbox select the link in the message from Facebook.
* **SMS code** : On your cell phone check for the 6-digit code and enter it on the screen.
* **Photo ID** : If requested upload a government-issued ID to the verification portal.
**Note:** Facebook encrypts your password and never displays it to other users.
You have now verified your identity and created your account.

## 4. Set Up Your Profile
Set up your profile to personalize your Facebook experience, help friends find you, and build trust within your network.
### 4.1 Add a Profile Photo
This procedure explains how to add a profile photo to your Facebook account.
**Prerequisites:**
* A Facebook account.
* An image file of a photo to use as your profile picture.

To add,
1. At the top of any page, click your profile icon.
2. Click **Add Profile Picture**.
3. To upload an image, do one of the following:
   * From your device, choose **Upload Photo**. 
   * To use your camera, choose **Take Photo**.
   **Note:** Use a clear, front-facing photo so friends can easily recognize you. Profile photos are always public.

4. Drag the image to reposition it within the cropping area, and click **Save**.
You have successfully added your profile photo.

### 4.2 Add a Cover Photo
This procedure explains how to add a cover photo to your Facebook account.
**Prerequisites:**
* A Facebook account.
* An image file of a photo to use as your cover picture.

To add,
1. Navigate to your profile page, on the large banner area click the camera icon.
2. Select **Upload Photo** or choose from your existing Facebook photos.
3. Reposition the image and click **Save Changes**.
You have successfully added your cover photo.

### 4.3 Complete Your Bio
This procedure explains how to complete your profile.
**Prerequisites:**
* A Facebook account.

To complete, 
1. On your profile page, click **Edit profile**.
2. Complete the profile by filling in the following sections:

| Section | What to include |
| --- | --- |
| Work | Current employer, job title, and previous positions |
| Education | High school, college, or university attended |
| Current city | Where you live now |
| Hometown | Where you grew up |
| Relationship status | Single, in a relationship, married, etc. (optional) |
| Bio | A short description of yourself (up to 101 characters) |
| Pronouns | How you identify (optional, visible to friends or everyone) |
| Websites and social links | Personal websites like LinkedIn and Instagram. |

**Note:** Each profile field has its own privacy control. You choose who sees each piece of information.
You have successfully completed your profile.

## 5. Manage Privacy and Security Settings
Managing privacy settings helps you to control who can see your content. Review privacy settings before adding friends or posting content to manage visibility. Proper configuration ensures that only intended audiences view personal data. This process protects the user from unwanted interactions and data mining. Users must understand how each setting affects their profile presence. Careful management of these controls is a fundamental part of account maintenance.

Benefits of managing privacy settings:
* Prevent unauthorized content viewing.
* Control digital footprint and identity.
* Reduce spam and harassment likelihood.

### 5.1 Access Privacy Settings
This procedure explains how to access your privacy settings.
**Prerequisites:**
* A Facebook account.
To access,

1. On the top right corner, click the down-arrow icon.
2. Go to **Settings & Privacy** and select **Settings**.
3. On the left pane, click **Privacy**.
You have successfully accessed your privacy settings.

### 5.2 Privacy Control Settings and Recommended Values
This procedure explains how to configure your privacy settings.
**Prerequisites:**
* A Facebook account.
The following table lists key privacy controls and recommended values.
| Setting | Menu | Recommended Value |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Future post visibility | On the left pane, click **Privacy** and select **Your Activity** | Friends |
| Friend request settings | On the left pane, click **Privacy** and select **How People Find You** | Friends of Friends |
| Email lookup visibility | On the left pane, click **Privacy** and select **How People Find You** | Friends |
| Phone number lookup visibility | On the left pane, click **Privacy** and select **How People Find You** | Friends |
| Search engine linking to profile | On the left pane, click **Privacy** and select **How People Find You** | Off |
| Profile picture visibility | On the left pane, click **Your Profile** and select **Edit** | Friends |
You have successfully configured your privacy settings.

### 5.3 Enable Two-Factor Authentication (2FA)
This procedure explains how to enable two-factor authentication (2FA).
**Prerequisites:**
* A Facebook account.
To enable,

1. Go to **Settings > Security and Login**.
2. Under **Two-Factor Authentication**, click **Edit**.
3. Select your preferred method: **Authentication App** (recommended) or **SMS**.
4. Follow the prompts to complete setup.
You have successfully enabled two-factor authentication.

## 6. Find Friends
This procedure explains how to find friends on Facebook.
**Prerequisites:**

To find friends, use one of the below options:

## 7. Manage Friend Requests

### 7.1 Accept a request
This procedure explains how to accept a friend request.
**Note:** Add only individuals whom you know. Accepting unknown friend requests can expose your posts and personal information to strangers.

**Prerequisites:**
* A Facebook account.
* A friend request.
To accept,

1. On the top navigation, click the Friends icon.
2. On the Friends page, in the left pane, click **Requests**.
3. On the **Requests** screen, against the request you want to accept, click **Confirm**.
The person will get a notification that you accepted their request and are now friends.

### 7.2 Decline a request
This procedure explains how to decline a friend request.
**Prerequisites:**
* A Facebook account.
* A friend request.
To decline,

1. On the top navigation, click the Friends icon.
2. On the Friends page, in the left pane, click **Requests**.
3. On the **Requests** screen, under the "Pending" section, find the request you want to decline. 
4. Click **Delete Request**. 
The request will be removed and the person will not get a notification.

### 7.3 Remove a friend
This procedure explains how to remove a friend.
**Prerequisites:**
* A Facebook account.
* A friend.
To remove,
1. On the top of the screen, in the search bar, enter the name of a friend.
2. Select the friend from the search results.   
3. Click on the friend name. The profile page of friend will open.
4. On the top navigation, click **Friends**.
5. On the pop-up window, click **Unfriend**.
6. Click **OK**.
The person will no longer appear in your friend list. The person will not get a notification.



## 8. Understand Your News Feed

The News Feed is the main page that appears after you log in. Use the News Feed to view friend posts, followed Pages, joined Groups, and sponsored content.The Facebook algorithm ranks content by your interactions. Posts from people you engage with most appear higher in the feed.

## 9. Create a Post
This procedure explains how to create a post.
**Prerequisites:**
* A Facebook account.

To create,

1. On the top of your News Feed or profile, click the **What is on your mind?** box.
2. Enter your text. You can also add one or more of the following:
   * **Photo/Video** : Upload media from your device
   * **Feeling/Activity** : Share your mood or what you are doing
   * **Tag People** : Mention friends so they are linked in the post
   * **Check In** : Share your current location
   * **Background Color** : Add a colored background to text-only posts
3. Select your audience: Friends, Public, Only Me, or a custom list.
4. Click **Post**.
The post will be published and visible to your selected audience.

## 10. Understand Post Privacy Options
This procedure explains how to understand post privacy options.
**Prerequisites:**
* A Facebook account.
The following table lists post privacy options.

| Audience Setting | Description |
| --- | --- |
| Public | Anyone on or off Facebook, including people not logged in |
| Friends | Only your confirmed Facebook friends |
| Friends except | All friends minus specific people you exclude |
| Specific friends | Only the people you manually select |
| Only me | Visible only to you. Useful for drafts or private notes |
| Custom | Combine any of the above with include/exclude logic |
You have successfully understood post privacy options.

## 11. Manage Facebook Groups

Facebook Groups are community spaces built around shared interests, causes, or affiliations.

### 11.1 Find Facebook Groups
This procedure explains how to find Facebook groups.
**Prerequisites:**
* A Facebook account.

To find Facebook groups, use one of the below options:

* **Groups Menu** : On the left pane, click **Groups**.
* **Search Bar** : At the top of the page, use the search bar to search for groups by name or topic.
* **Discover** : In the Groups section, click **Discover** to browse categories like Suggested for You or Popular Near You.
You have successfully found Facebook groups.

### 11.2 Join Facebook Groups
This procedure explains how to join Facebook groups.
**Prerequisites:**
* A Facebook account.
* A Facebook group.
To join,

1. On the left pane, click **Groups**.
2. (optional) On the top of the page, use the search bar to search for groups by name or topic.
3. Click on the group you want to join. If it's a public group, to its page, you can see posts and the group description.
4. Click **Join**.
   **Notes:** If the group is public, you will be added immediately. If it is private or closed, your request will be sent to the admins for approval.
   **Notes:** If you are joining as a Facebook Page, you will be asked to select whether to join as your personal profile or your Page. Not all groups allow Pages to join. This depends on the groups settings.
The group will be added to your list of joined groups. You will see posts from the group in your feed.

### 11.3 Create a Group
This procedure explains how to create a group.
**Prerequisites:**
* A Facebook account.

To create,
1. On the left pane, click **Groups**.
2. At the top of the page, click **Create Group**.
3. Enter the group name and select **Next**.
4. Select a group type and click **Next**.
5. Enter a group description and click **Next**.
6. Choose who can post and click **Next**.
7. Review your group information and click **Create**.
The group will be created and you will be taken to the group dashboard.

## 14. Troubleshoot Common Issues
This procedure explains how to troubleshoot common issues.
The following table lists common issues and their solutions.
| Problem | Solution |
| --- | --- |
| Forgot your password | On the login page, select **Forgot Password**. Enter your email or phone, and select the reset link to continue. |
| Locked account | The system locks accounts for policy violations or shows suspicious activity. Go to facebook.com/login/identify to begin the account recovery process. |
| Unable to find a friend | Ask your friend to  check their privacy settings to ensure others can search for them. |
| Missing post from feed | A post may be missing if it was deleted or if the audience settings excluded you. Ask the poster directly for help. |
| Irrelevant News Feed | Use the three-dot menu on posts to select **Hide Post** or **Snooze [Name]** for 30 days to improve feed relevance. |
| Excessive notifications | Go to **Settings** and select **Notifications** to customize or reduce the notification types you receive. |
| Unauthorized login | Go to **Settings** and select **Security and Login** then choose **Where You’re Logged In**. Log out of any unrecognized sessions and change your password immediately. |

## 15. Stay Safe on Facebook

**Important:** Facebook will never ask for your password in email or Messenger. Do not trust messages that ask for your sign in information.

Follow these best practices to keep your account and personal information safe:

* Use a unique strong password and change it ever 6 to 12 months.
* Enable two-factor authentication. Refer to Section 5.3.
* Review your active sessions regularly. Refer to Section 5.4.
* Do not click links in messages that the user did not request.
* Report suspicious accounts, spam, or inappropriate content using the three-dot menu on any post or profile.
* Be cautious about sharing your phone number, home address, or daily routine publicly.
* Use the Privacy Checkup tool  to audit your settings every few months. Refer to Section 5.5.
